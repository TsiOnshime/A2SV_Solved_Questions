# [Least Prime Factor](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/least-prime-factor5216/1)
## Easy
Given an integer n, find the least prime factor (LPF) of every integer from 1 to n.
The least prime factor of an integer x is the smallest prime number that divides x.
Return an array of size n+1, using 1-based indexing, where the ith element stores the least prime factor of the (i + 1)th integer.
For i = 1, the least prime factor is defined as 1.
Examples:
Input: n = 6
Output: [1, 2, 3, 2, 5, 2]&nbsp;
Explanation: least prime factor of 1 = 1,
least prime factor of 2 = 2,
least prime factor of 3 = 3,
least prime factor of 4 = 2,
least prime factor of 5 = 5,
least prime factor of 6 = 2.

Input: n = 4
Output:&nbsp;[1, 2, 3, 2]
Explanation: least prime factor of 1 = 1,
least prime factor of 2 = 2,
least prime factor of 3 = 3,
least prime factor of 4 = 2.
